StreamRoute

Send player connections through a local proxy.

Reading routing status…

Requires an existing local SOCKS5/HTTP proxy. Cloudflare HTTPS DNS-only does not provide one. No proxy settings change until you press Enable.

Proxy address: 127.0.0.1. Use the protocol and port of your local proxy.

Matching hosts and their subdomains use the proxy in every tab. The page, iframe, and media CDN can use different hosts. Review this list before enabling.

What this does

Chrome sends matching web connections to the local proxy instead of directly through your ISP. HTTPS still uses TLS; an organization's proxy may inspect TLS if its policy and trusted certificate enable that. DNS-only cannot carry video traffic; the proxy must forward it through a real tunnel.

Stop removes only StreamRoute's proxy override and reveals your previous Chrome/system configuration. A stopped or unavailable proxy produces a proxy connection error; matching traffic does not silently fall back to a direct route.

All Chrome web traffic also covers separate video CDN hosts. It covers proxyable web connections, not every browser protocol or other apps. The local proxy's own limits and server blocks still apply.
